# Shadowdark-inspired style guide

Use a dark fantasy tabletop illustration style inspired by old-school dungeon RPG art.

## Core look

- Strong inked outlines
- Slightly rough hand-drawn feel
- Stylized rather than realistic
- Moody and atmospheric
- Readable silhouettes
- Limited but rich color palette
- Slight parchment / printed-book feeling
- Mild texture, not glossy
- High contrast between character and background

## For sprites

- Keep forms simple and readable at small size
- Avoid excessive detail
- Preserve clear outlines
- Use compact, iconic shapes
- Use minimal shading with 1–2 tone steps
- Avoid photorealism
- Avoid soft blurry rendering
- Avoid modern UI icon look
- Avoid anime proportions unless explicitly requested

## Player design

- Adventurer silhouette
- Slightly grim or tense
- Dynamic pose readability
- Fantasy-clothing hints are acceptable
- Keep the sprite small-game friendly

## Obstacle design

- Dungeon / ruin / grim fantasy world feel
- Stone, spikes, ritual pillars, broken masonry, or iron structures
- Must remain modular and readable
- Keep collision silhouette visually clear

## Avoid

- Sci-fi look
- Cartoon comedy style
- Clean vector-flat corporate illustration
- Photorealistic rendering
- Overly bright saturated palette
